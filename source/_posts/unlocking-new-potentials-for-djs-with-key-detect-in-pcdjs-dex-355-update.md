## Unlocking New Potentials for DJs with Key Detect in PCDJ's DEX 3.5.5 Update

[![Key mixing in DEX 3](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/keydetection-mixinginkey.jpg?resize=530%2C293&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/keydetection-mixinginkey.jpg?fit=530%2C293&ssl=1 "What is Key Mixing in DEX 3")

DEX 3.5.5 is now equipped with **musical key detection** for [harmonic mixing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmonic%5Fmixing). This means **DEX 3 can calculate and display key for all your audio, video and karaoke files.**

[TRY DEX 3.5.5 WITH KEY DETECTION ![Key Displayed In DEX 3 DJ Software](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/keymixing-dex3-image2.jpg?fit=280%2C280&ssl=1 "DEX 3 with Key Detection")](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

Under the general tab in DEX 3’s options tick the box for ‘**musical key detection**‘ and when you load a track into any deck, key will be calculated automagically. Key is then displayed next to the key step buttons located in any of DEX 3’s decks (default 3.5 skins only). You’ll be provided 2 key options per track: **A modified Camelot (pre “/”) and the musical notation (post “/”).**

#### So now that key is available in each deck and in the DEX 3 browser, how do you benefit from it?

Mixing songs in the same key, otherwise **mixing tracks harmonically**, has become a popular technique with pro DJs in recent years. Tracks simply blend better when in the same key, providing the ability to **create on-the-fly mashups without key clashes**. Mixing songs on beat is fundamental, but if the melodies don’t intertwine seamlessly the result is clashing keys and a poor quality mix.

As [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmonic%5Fmixing) explains it:

_“The primary goal of **harmonic mixing** is to create a smooth transition between songs. Songs in the same key do not generate a [dissonant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consonance%5Fand%5Fdissonance "Consonance and dissonance") tone when mixed. This technique enables DJs to create a harmonious and consonant [mashup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mashup%5F%28music%29 "Mashup (music)") with any [music genre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music%5Fgenre "Music genre").”_

[DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) also provides a **key stepper for pitch shifting**, so you can change the key of one track to better match another.

[![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/dex3-banner.png?fit=300%2C187&ssl=1 "dex3-banner")](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/dex3-banner.png?fit=762%2C476&ssl=1)

There are many advanced **key mixing** techniques we’ll touch on in future blog posts, but our suggestion to try your hand now at **mixing in key** is to select two tracks of the same key (IE: 9A and 9A) and have a play. You’ll immediately notice how very easy it is to **mix songs harmonically** in key with DEX 3.5.5, and how **mixing in key** can open the door to exciting creative possibilities during all your DJ sets.
