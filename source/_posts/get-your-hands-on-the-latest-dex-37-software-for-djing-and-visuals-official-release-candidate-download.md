## Get Your Hands on the Latest DEX 3.7 Software for DJing & Visuals - Official Release Candidate Download.

[![DEX 3.7 DJ And Video Mixing Software Download](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/dex37releasecandidate-cover.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/dex37releasecandidate-cover.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "DEX 3.7 DJ And Video Mixing Software Download")

**Public Beta 2 (release candidate) of DEX 3.7.0.1 for both Mac and Windows is ready for a test spin!**

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![DEX 3 with Video, Text And Image Overlays](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/text-video-image-overlays-dex3.jpg?fit=300%2C169&ssl=1 "DEX 3 With Video, Text and Image overlays")](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/text-video-image-overlays-dex3.jpg?fit=1030%2C579&ssl=1)

As outlined in the [previous 3.7 beta post](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) from last week, DEX 3.7 introduces the ability to **create beautiful multi-layer text, image and video overlays** that you can display on screen for your audience – while mixing music videos or audio.

The DEX 3.7 beta 2 update, which is a release candidate, can be downloaded below and includes a couple tweaks to the new overlay capabilities – notably the ability to **create overlays before sending live** to the audience in the event you want to design them on the fly instead of in pre-production.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/hercules-djcontrol-jogvision-top-right-angle_960x540.jpg?fit=300%2C169&ssl=1 "Hercules")](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/hercules-djcontrol-jogvision-top-right-angle%5F960x540.jpg?fit=960%2C540&ssl=1)

The PCDJ lab has been anything but quiet this past week as development has also found time to add **plug-and-play support for the [DJ Control JogVision](https://www.hercules.com/us/advanced-controllers/bdd/p/244/djcontrol-jogvision/)** — Hercules DJ’s most advanced and feature-rich [DJ controller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DJ%5Fcontroller) to date.

We hope DJs and VDJs are as excited about the freshly added overlay features as we are — and we think you will truly going to love what we have in store for 3.8.

**Please note:** This build of DEX 3.7 is a “release candidate” – which means our development team feels it’s road-ready for live performance. With that said, we highly suggest testing DEX 3.7 beta 2 before going live to make sure everything play’s nicely!

#### All Changes Included In DEX 3.7.0.1 (Public Beta 2)

**Changes Included in Previous Beta 1:**

* Video Graphics Overlays (add custom text, images, videos to the video screen. “show logo” on screen)
* Custom background (image or video) for Karaoke Singers List
* “Disable Pulselocker” setting available under the general tab in options
* Completely re-implemented Karaoke Singers List
* New skin/scripting actions: “show\_video\_overlays” skin action, “videoOverlays” MIDI/skin scripting
* Several bugs squashed

**Additional Changes/Fixes in Beta 2:**

* New controller supported: [Hercules DJControl Jogvision](https://www.hercules.com/us/advanced-controllers/bdd/p/244/djcontrol-jogvision/)
* Multiple fixes for the [Denon MC-4000](http://www.denondj.com/products/view/mc4000) controller script
* Text, Image and Video overlays are created disabled (edit overlays before making them visible – tick the box next to the item to show on video output)
* Fixed video overlays bug – loading certain images on Mac would not work
* Fixed video overlays bug – loading images on Windows would reverse colors
* Several small bug fixes
