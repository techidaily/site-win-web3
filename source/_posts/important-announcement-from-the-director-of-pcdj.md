## Important Announcement From the Director of PCDJ

[![](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/thank-you.jpg?resize=845%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/thank-you.jpg?fit=1030%2C534&ssl=1 "Many people together in front of harsh lights, lens flare is visible.")

**As we begin our 24th year at PCDJ, we want to say thank you.**

We’re off to a great start. The karaoke and DJ software projects and platforms we are working on now will continue, and the features and services they support will only get better. We will innovate more, publish more, and connect with our community more in 2023.

PCDJ users have a lot to look forward to, beginning with the finalization of our massive tune-up to our platforms audio engine, which is 2/3’s of the way completed in the latest [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and [LYRX](http://www.lyrxkaraoke.com/) versions. What will it mean for DJ’s and KJ’s? The most stable environment available with superior sound quality, as well as the foundation for amazing developments and features to come. Development has a few nice surprises up their collective sleeve that you’ll be able to enjoy this year, all with the singular goal to assist you in creating unique, memorable events for clients and party goers.

Your continued support drives this mission. As we kick off another new year, we are thankful for you and all that you’ve helped us achieve. Here’s to the future!

Happy 2023!

**Ryan Sherr**  
**PCDJ Director**
